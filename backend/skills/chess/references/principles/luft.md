# Luft — an escape square for the king

## When to use

Your king is castled behind unmoved pawns (f2-g2-h2), the position has open
or soon-to-open files to your first rank, and your rooks may leave the back
rank. Especially urgent when heavy pieces are still on the board.

## The idea

"Luft" (air) is a pawn move — usually h3, sometimes g3 — that gives the
castled king a flight square. Without it, any enemy rook or queen landing on
your first rank with check is instantly mate
([[patterns/mating-patterns/back-rank-mate]]). One quiet tempo removes a
whole family of mates permanently.

## What to do

- When you have a free move and your back rank is guarded by only one piece
  (or none), play h3. Do it *before* the threat exists — afterwards is often
  too late.
- After making luft, a back-rank check is just a check: your king steps to
  h2 and the "mate" evaporates.
- Conversely, when attacking: an enemy king *without* luft is a standing
  target — count the defenders of their back rank every time a file opens.

## Watch out for

- h3 weakens g3 and invites ...Bxh3 sacrifices when the opponent's pieces
  point at your king — make luft in quiet moments, not under attack.
- Luft on g3 (instead of h3) can be undone by a queen landing on h3.
- Don't spend the tempo when your rooks firmly control the back rank and
  have no reason to leave it.

## Examples

No luft: `3r2k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/6K1 b` — 1...Rd1#, game over (verified
mate). With luft: `3r2k1/5ppp/8/8/8/7P/5PP1/6K1 b` — 1...Rd1+ is met by
Kh2 and White plays on (verified). The difference is the single move h3.
