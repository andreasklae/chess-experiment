# Principles — Index

Short rules of thumb you check a move *against*. These are the "don't hang
pieces", "when attacked you can defend / move / counter-threaten", "look for
passed pawns when down material" level — bite-sized heuristics, not plans.

**Read a page here when:** you have a candidate move and want a sanity
check, or you're unsure of a basic guideline. Principles are quick to
consult; a plan is what you build in [`../strategy/`](../strategy/index.md).
