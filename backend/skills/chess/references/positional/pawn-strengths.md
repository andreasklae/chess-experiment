# Pawn strengths

## When to use
You have (or face) a passed pawn, a pawn majority, connected pawns, or a pawn
chain, and want to use it — or stop it.

## The idea
- **Passed pawn** — no enemy pawn on its file or the two adjacent files ahead of
  it; nothing but pieces can stop it promoting. Capablanca: a passed pawn is
  *very weak or very strong*, and **its strength grows as it advances and as
  pieces come off the board**. A **protected/connected** passer is stronger; an
  **outside** passer (far from the kings) decoys the enemy king in the endgame.
- **Connected pawns / majority** — pawns on adjacent files defend each other and
  advance as a phalanx. Capablanca: **pawns are strongest side by side on the
  same rank.** A majority on a wing can manufacture a passed pawn.
- **Pawn chain** — pawns on a diagonal, each defending the next. It has a
  **base** (rearmost, the root) and a **head** (most advanced).

## What to do
**Using your passed pawn / majority:**
- Advance it; **support it from behind with a rook** (rook belongs *behind*
  passed pawns — yours and theirs).
- In the endgame the **KING escorts the pawn** — an extra piece is not what
  wins K+P races; bring the king.
- Keep connected pawns **together**; advance a majority to create one passer
  ("the candidate" is the pawn the enemy has no pawn in front of).
- Many passers vs a lone king: cut the king off with a rook and push them.

**Stopping the enemy's:**
- **Blockade** the square in front with a piece (knight ideal) — stops the pawn
  and sits safe.
- Get your **rook behind** their passed pawn; or win it; or trade into a
  structure where their majority can't make a passer.

**Pawn chain:** attack the **base** (its root weakness); play on the side your
chain points toward.

## Watch out for
- A passed pawn is only as good as its support — a lone runner gets blockaded and
  rounded up. Don't push it into a blockade without backup.
- Don't trade your last piece that holds a blockade on the enemy passer.

then your king eats the other wing. See [[endgames/king-pawn-endings]] and the
