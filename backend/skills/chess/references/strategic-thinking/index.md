# Strategic Thinking — Index

Long-term planning and the mental models for forming a plan from a
position: how to decide *what you are trying to do* over the next 10–20
moves, which imbalances matter, where your pieces belong.

**Read a page here when:** the position is quiet or unclear and you need a
plan, not a single move. This is the "what's my plan?" folder. For a quick
rule of thumb instead, use [`../principles/`](../principles/index.md).
