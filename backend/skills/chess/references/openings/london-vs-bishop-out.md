# London: Black's bishop comes out early (...Bf5 / ...Bg4)

## When to use
Black develops the light-squared bishop **outside** its pawn chain before ...e6 —
either **...Bf5** (mirroring your own London bishop) or **...Bg4** (pinning your
Nf3). Both are solid, respected replies; you don't win by force, but you have clear
plans, and Black has a concrete trap to fall into.

## The idea
Black's point is to solve the "bad light bishop" before locking it in with ...e6.
You have nothing to fear — just develop naturally. The one thing that changes is the
**...Bg4 pin** on f3: don't let it win the knight or wreck your kingside.

## What to do
- **vs ...Bf5:** develop as usual — **c3, Nf3, Nbd2, Be2/Bd3, O-O**. Don't rush to
  chase the bishop. You can play for **Ne5** and the kingside as normal, or **Qb3**
  hitting b7 and d5 (the bishop no longer guards b7).
- **vs ...Bg4:** break or ignore the pin. **Nbd2** first (unpins nothing but keeps
  developing), **Be2** (offers to trade the pinner), or **h3** asking the bishop's
  intention (…Bh5 g4? only if you're ready; usually …Bxf3 gives you the bishop pair).
- **The trap — Qa4!** In lines with **...Bg4** and **...Nc6**, playing **Qa4** pins
  the c6-knight to the king. If Black then blunders **...Bd6??**, you win with
  **Ba6!** — after **...Bxf4 Bxb7** your bishop FORKS the a8-rook and the c6-knight,
  winning material.

## Watch out for
- **Don't hang your own Nf3 to the ...Bg4 pin.** If your queen leaves d1 with the
  knight pinned and the d4-pawn under pressure, calculate ...Bxf3 first.
- **...Bf5 is solid, not bad** — don't overpress trying to "punish" it; just play a
  good London and aim for Ne5 / e4.
- The Qa4 trap only bites if Black plays a careless ...Bd6 — don't expect it; play it
  as a normal position and take the trap only if it's really there (check Ba6 wins).

## Examples (verified)
- Mirror: `1.d4 d5 2.Bf4 Nf6 3.e3 Bf5` — Black is rock-solid; develop and aim for Ne5.
- Trap: `1.d4 d5 2.Bf4 Nf6 3.e3 c5 4.c3 Nc6 5.Nf3 Bg4 6.Nbd2 e6 7.Qa4 Bd6?? 8.Ba6!
  Bxf4 9.Bxb7` — Bxb7 forks Ra8 + Nc6, winning material.
