# Openings — Index

Synthesised opening theory: how to handle the first phase of the game,
organised by opening or by the kind of position you're steering toward.

**Read a page here when:** you are in roughly the first 10 moves, the
position matches a named opening, or you're unsure what to develop toward.

## Pages

We play the **London System** as White. Start at the hub page, then jump to the
situational page that matches what Black just did.

| Page | When it applies | Read with |
|------|-----------------|-----------|
| [london-system](london-system.md) | **Start here.** You are White in the opening — the London setup (d4, Bf4, e3, Nf3, Bd3/Be2, c3, Nbd2, O-O) and the core **Ne5** plan. Routes to the pages below. | principles/opening-principles |
| [london-vs-qb6](london-vs-qb6.md) | Black played **...Qb6** hitting your loose **b2-pawn** (often with ...c5). The right defence depends on move order — **Qb3 only works after you've played c3**. | tactics/traps |
| [london-vs-kings-indian](london-vs-kings-indian.md) | Black fianchettoes **...g6, ...Bg7, ...d6, ...O-O** (King's Indian setup). Put the bishop on **e2 not d3**, add **h3**. | principles/center-control |
| [london-vs-nh5](london-vs-nh5.md) | Black plays **...Nh5** to trade your **f4 dark-squared bishop**. Retreat to **h2** (if h3 in) or **g5**, or allow ...Nxg3 **hxg3** to open the h-file. | — |
| [london-bxh7-greek-gift](london-bxh7-greek-gift.md) | Your **Bd3 points at h7**, Black has castled, and you're tempted by **Bxh7+**. It WINS only under strict conditions (no Black Nf6, Ng5+ ready, queen reaches the h-file). Read BEFORE sacrificing. | mates/index, positional/king-safety |
