# Openings — Index

Synthesised opening theory: how to handle the first phase of the game,
organised by opening or by the kind of position you're steering toward.

**Read a page here when:** you are in roughly the first 10 moves, the
position matches a named opening, or you're unsure what to develop toward.

## Pages

We play the **London System** as White. Start at the hub page, then jump to the
situational page that matches what Black just did.

| Page | When it applies | Read with |

| [london-ne5-attack](london-ne5-attack.md) | Setup done, Black castled — **now what?** The main plan: **Ne5** outpost → **f4** → **Rf3–h3** lift → **h4–h5** storm → **Bxh7+**. Read when developed and looking for a plan/attack. | openings/london-bxh7-greek-gift |
| [london-central-break](london-central-break.md) | The London's OTHER plan: the **e3–e4** central break (Qe2, Re1, e4), and the **pawn structures** (Carlsbad → Nd3; doubled f-pawns) that tell you which plan to pick. | principles/center-control |
| [london-vs-bishop-out](london-vs-bishop-out.md) | Black develops the light bishop early — **...Bf5** (mirror) or **...Bg4** (pin on f3). Solid for Black, but the **Qa4 → Ba6** trap punishes a careless ...Bd6. | tactics/pins-and-skewers |
| [london-vs-early-c5](london-vs-early-c5.md) | Black hits **d4 with an early ...c5** (before your c3). Support d4, or the **Nc3–Nb5** gambit vs ...Qxb2, or **push d5** into a favourable Benoni. | openings/london-vs-qb6 |
| [london-jobava](london-jobava.md) | The **Jobava London** (2.Nc3, then Bf4) — a SHARPER sibling with Nb5, h4/g4 storms, O-O-O. A different plan; don't mix it with the quiet London. | openings/london-system |
| [london-vs-qb6](london-vs-qb6.md) | Black played **...Qb6** hitting your loose **b2-pawn** (often with ...c5). The right defence depends on move order — **Qb3 only works after you've played c3**. | tactics/traps |
| [london-vs-kings-indian](london-vs-kings-indian.md) | Black fianchettoes **...g6, ...Bg7, ...d6, ...O-O** (King's Indian setup). Put the bishop on **e2 not d3**, add **h3**. | principles/center-control |
| [london-vs-nh5](london-vs-nh5.md) | Black plays **...Nh5** to trade your **f4 dark-squared bishop**. Retreat to **h2** (if h3 in) or **g5**, or allow ...Nxg3 **hxg3** to open the h-file. | — |
| [london-bxh7-greek-gift](london-bxh7-greek-gift.md) | Your **Bd3 points at h7**, Black has castled, and you're tempted by **Bxh7+**. It WINS only under strict conditions (no Black Nf6, Ng5+ ready, queen reaches the h-file). Read BEFORE sacrificing. | mates/index, positional/king-safety |

## Routing

- **Which page?** Read `london-system` first (the hub) — it routes you to the
  situational page for whatever Black just did, and to the two plan pages
  (`london-ne5-attack`, `london-central-break`) once you're developed.
- Need a *principle* rather than concrete theory ("develop pieces, don't
  move the same piece twice") → [`../principles/`](../principles/index.md).
- Judging a pawn structure the opening produced (isolated d-pawn, doubled pawns) →
  [`../positional/`](../positional/index.md).
- A tactic appeared in the opening (fork, pin, hanging piece) →
  [`../tactics/`](../tactics/index.md).
