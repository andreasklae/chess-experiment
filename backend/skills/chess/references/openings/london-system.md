# London System (White)

## When to use
You are White, it is the opening (roughly moves 1–10), and you want one reliable
setup you can aim for against almost anything Black does. The London is solid,
low-risk, and reaches the **same structure** nearly every game — learn it once.

## The idea
Build this exact structure (the "London triangle" d4-e3-c3, the bishop OUTSIDE the
pawn chain on f4), then put a knight on **e5**:

- **1.d4**, then **2.Bf4** — get the dark-squared bishop out to f4 *before* e3, so it
  is not trapped behind your own pawns. (Play 2.Bf4 early, not Nf3 first.)
- **3.e3** (supports d4, opens the f1-bishop), **4.Nf3**, **5.Bd3** (aims at h7 — see
  the Greek-gift page), **6.Nbd2**, **7.c3** (supports d4), and **castle short (O-O)**.
- Add **h3** when useful, to give the f4-bishop the h2 retreat square if it is attacked.

The plan after the setup: land a knight on **e5** (supported by the d4-pawn, the
bishop, and often f2-f4), then play on the kingside. The Ne5 outpost is the heart of
the London — a knight there pressures f7/g6, supports a kingside attack, and makes the
"boring" London genuinely dangerous. If Black ever takes on e5, recapture **dxe5** to
kick the f6-knight away.

## What to do — route by Black's reply
- **Black plays ...c5 and ...Qb6** (hitting your d4 and the b2-pawn) → this is the
  main test and the answer DEPENDS ON MOVE ORDER. Read `openings/london-vs-qb6`.
- **Black fianchettoes ...g6 and ...Bg7** (King's Indian setup) → read
  `openings/london-vs-kings-indian`.
- **Black plays ...Nh5 to trade your f4-bishop** → read `openings/london-vs-nh5`.
- **Your bishop is on d3, Black has castled and has NO knight on f6** → a **Bxh7+**
  sacrifice may be on. Read `openings/london-bxh7-greek-gift` BEFORE playing it.
- **Otherwise** (a quiet QGD-like setup with ...d5 ...e6) → complete the setup, play
  Ne5, then expand on the kingside.

## Watch out for
- **Don't play e3 before Bf4** — the bishop gets stuck behind the pawn chain (the whole
  point of the London is the bishop OUTSIDE it).
- **The b2-pawn** is loose once the bishop leaves c1 — see `openings/london-vs-qb6`.
- **Don't sacrifice on h7 just because the bishop points there** — it only works under
  strict conditions (Greek-gift page). A premature Bxh7+ just loses a piece.
