# Opening fundamentals

**Goal of the opening:** get all your pieces into play quickly, control the
centre, and make your king safe — in the fewest moves. The side with more pieces
ready where the fight breaks out wins (Lasker). Do these every move while pieces
are still at home.

## Do
- **Develop a NEW piece almost every move.** Knights first (to f3/c3 or f6/c6),
  then bishops to active squares. Aim: both knights, both bishops out, castled,
  rooks connected — then the opening is "done."
- **Fight for the centre.** Put a pawn on e4/d4 (e5/d5) and point pieces at the
  centre; a knight on f3 controls e5/d4.
- **Castle early** — usually by move ~6-10. A safe king lets you fight freely.
- **Make pawn moves that open lines for your pieces** (the e- and d-pawns open
  the queen and a bishop). Other pawn moves should be rare.

## Don't
- **Don't bring the queen out early** — it gets chased by developing moves and
  you lose tempo.
- **Don't move the same piece twice** in the opening without a concrete reason.
- **Don't make many pawn moves** (especially a3/h3/a6/h6) — each is a tempo not
  developing, and can create weaknesses.
- **Don't grab a pawn** if it costs development or king safety — in the opening a
  "free" pawn is often **bait** (a trap). Check the opponent's reply first
  ([[tactics/traps]]).
- **Don't trade a bishop for a knight** early without a reason (Capablanca).

## Watch for (every opening move)
- Did the opponent's move threaten something? (Answer it — don't develop blindly
  into a threat.) See [[fundamentals/every-move-checklist]] step 1.
- Is my intended developing move actually safe (no fork/pin waiting)? Verify with
  `chess__imagine_move`.
- A premature pin (…Bg4 / Bg5) can be punished — and so can falling for one; see
  the Légal trap in [[tactics/traps]].

## When the opening is over
Once you're developed and castled, switch to a plan: assess the position and pick
a target ([[positional/evaluate-position]], [[fundamentals/middlegame]]).

**One line:** *every move, develop a new piece toward the centre or castle —
unless you must answer a threat or you have a safe tactic.*
