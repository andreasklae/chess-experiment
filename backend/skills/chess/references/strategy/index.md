# Strategy — Index

Long-term planning: deciding *what you are trying to do* over the next
10–20 moves, and how to finish a game you are already winning. Read here
when the position is quiet or unclear and you need a plan, not a single
move. For a quick rule of thumb instead, see
[`../principles/`](../principles/index.md).

## Pages

| Read this when… | Page |
|---|---|
| you are clearly ahead in material and need to convert the win without drifting | [convert-advantage](convert-advantage.md) |
| the position is quiet/unclear, or your recent moves have been aimless and you need a plan | [make-a-plan](make-a-plan.md) |

*(Pawn-structure planning pages — isolated/passed/doubled pawns, chains,
majorities — are added by ingestion later.)*

## Routing

- The plan is concrete and tactical (a combination is there) → [`../tactics/`](../tactics/index.md).
- You are trying to checkmate → [`../mates/`](../mates/index.md).
- Few pieces left and it is technique → [`../endgames/`](../endgames/index.md).
